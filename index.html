VapoCept™ Ingredients

VapoCept™ is described online with different ingredient lists, so the safest approach is to use the most recently published label information rather than mixing formulas from different sites. A September 2026 label lists seven active ingredients: L-Arginine, L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract, L-Citrulline HCl, L-Citrulline Malate, Niacin, Beta-Alanine, and Calcium.

1

L-Arginine — 200 mg

L-Arginine is an amino acid involved in the body's nitric-oxide pathway. It is included to support normal circulation and blood-flow processes.
2

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract — 200 mg

This ingredient combines L-Arginine with alpha-ketoglutarate. It provides another form of arginine to complement the formula's overall amino-acid profile.
3

L-Citrulline HCl — 100 mg

L-Citrulline can be converted into L-Arginine in the body and is therefore associated with the nitric-oxide pathway and normal blood-flow support.
4

L-Citrulline Malate — 100 mg

This form combines citrulline with malate. Citrulline contributes to the arginine pathway, while malate is involved in normal cellular energy metabolism.
5

Niacin (Vitamin B3) — 7.5 mg NE

Niacin is an essential B vitamin involved in normal energy-yielding metabolism and nervous-system function. The listed serving provides about 47% of the Daily Value.
6

Beta-Alanine — 40 mg

Beta-alanine is an amino acid used by the body to produce carnosine. It contributes to the formula's overall amino-acid and cellular-support profile.
7

Calcium — 18 mg

Calcium, listed as calcium carbonate, is an essential mineral involved in normal cellular signaling and several other physiological functions.

Other Ingredients
The published label also lists hypromellose for the vegetable capsule, vegetable stearate, and silicon dioxide as manufacturing/flow aids.
